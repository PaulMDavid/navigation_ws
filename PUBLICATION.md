# Publication and Visibility

**Goals:** Publish the sensor data collected with our rover in a way that allows others to reuse and cite it. At the same time, we want to increase the visibility of our team and our work and create opportunities to connect with other researchers.

**Overview of the options:**
- **Repository:** Makes the data publicly available and can provide a DOI.
- **Data Paper:** Describes the dataset and is peer-reviewed.
- **Presentations, Posters and Workshops:** Bring the work to the research community and provide opportunities for networking and discussion.



[TU Berlin SZF](https://www.tu.berlin/en/ub/szf) offers support and guidance for TU members on publishing research data. Maybe they can also help with funding, visibility, etc. I still need to check their specific offers.

> **Note:** Information may change or be expanded as I gather more data.
The available budget also needs to be discussed.

## 2. Data Paper

**Article Processing Charge (APC)** = cost of publishing a paper

- [Data in Brief](https://www.sciencedirectelsevier.com/journal/Data-in-Brief) — APC: USD 1,600 (excluding taxes)
- [Data / MDPI](https://www.mdpi.com/journal/data) — APC: ~€1,700
- [Scientific Data](https://www.nature.com/sdata) — APC: €2,390; we may be able to publish for free:
  > "As an author at Technische Universität Berlin you may be able to publish in Scientific Data with 100% of the APC paid by your institution."

### What Should a Data Paper Include?

- Description of the rover platform, sensors, sensor positions, and configuration
- Calibration, timestamps and synchronization, coordinate frames
- Data collection conditions and test environments
- Quality checks, known errors, and limitations
- Processing steps and scripts
- Terms of use / license
